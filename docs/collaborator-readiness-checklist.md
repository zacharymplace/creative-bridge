# Collaborator Readiness Checklist

A lightweight self-assessment for collaborators who may want to participate in Creative Bridge projects.

This is not certification, verification, or endorsement.

## Role Clarity

- [ ] Your role lanes are clear.
- [ ] Your preferred mediums or formats are clear.
- [ ] Your contribution types are clear.

## AI-Adjacent Comfort

- [ ] You state what kinds of AI-assisted projects you are open to considering.
- [ ] You state what kinds of AI-assisted workflows you do not want to support.
- [ ] You clarify whether you are open to revising, redrawing, editing, producing, or consulting on AI-assisted prototypes.

## Compensation and Scope

- [ ] You state whether you consider paid work.
- [ ] You state whether you consider speculative work.
- [ ] You state whether you consider advisory conversations.
- [ ] You state basic expectations around scope, revisions, timeline, and terms.

## Credit and Attribution

- [ ] You state how you expect to be credited.
- [ ] You state what public claims require approval.
- [ ] You state that no project may imply your attachment without agreement.

## Contact and Boundaries

- [ ] Contact expectations are clear.
- [ ] Public tagging boundaries are clear.
- [ ] You do not invite pressure-based outreach.
- [ ] You define what should remain private or opt-in.

## Non-Marketplace Behavior

- [ ] No public profile is implied.
- [ ] No project attachment is implied.
- [ ] No messaging layer is implied.
- [ ] No hiring transaction is implied.
