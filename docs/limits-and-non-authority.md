# Limits & Non-Authority Statement

Version: 2.4.0

The Creative Bridge is a public standard and review system.

It is not an authority, certification body, legal shield, audit program, rights registry, or ethics approval service.

## The standard does not

- certify AI tools
- verify training data
- legalize projects
- approve creators
- endorse projects
- replace legal review
- guarantee ethical status
- resolve labor fairness

## Proper use

Use the standard to:

- make process, boundaries, and risks more visible
- improve collaborator-facing clarity before outreach
- separate disclosure from endorsement
- document decisions and rejected patterns
- ask better review questions

## Misuse warning

If this standard is used to imply certification, legality, endorsement, safety, or moral approval, it is being misused.
