# Reviewer Brief Pack

Version: 1.7.0

The Reviewer Brief Pack gives different reviewers a focused way to critique The Creative Bridge.

The goal is targeted criticism, not vague approval.

## Artists

Focus: Review whether the standard protects artistic labor, avoids style impersonation loopholes, and gives enough weight to credit, consent, and human craft.

Ask: Where could this still allow artist replacement, vague credit, style laundering, or AI-assisted work being passed off as human-made?

## Writers

Focus: Review whether the language is clear, principled, specific, and hard to misuse.

Ask: Where does the wording create loopholes, overclaim authority, soften a boundary, or fail to explain the actual standard?

## Designers

Focus: Review whether the site structure, navigation, and hierarchy make the standard easy to understand without turning it into SaaS gloss.

Ask: Where does the interface hide important boundaries, over-polish the seriousness, or make the review path harder than it should be?

## Developers

Focus: Review the static architecture, docs mirroring, schemas, QA script, release structure, and deployment path.

Ask: Where is the repo fragile, overcomplicated, under-tested, or likely to break as more standards/docs/routes are added?

## Skeptics

Focus: Review whether the project actually resists the harms it names or merely presents a cleaner wrapper around the same incentives.

Ask: Where does this still become AI laundering, marketplace drift, fake legitimacy, review theater, or platform capture?

## Collaborators

Focus: Review whether contributor boundaries, role requests, credit expectations, and opt-in discovery rules are strong enough.

Ask: Where could a collaborator be pressured, under-credited, implied as attached, or pulled into unclear scope?

## Creators

Focus: Review whether the standard gives creators a realistic path from AI-assisted prototype toward better human collaboration.

Ask: Where does the process feel useful, where does it feel too heavy, and where could it unintentionally reward shortcuts?
