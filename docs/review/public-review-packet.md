# Public Review Packet

Version: 1.1.0

The Public Review Packet defines how people can review The Creative Bridge standard without turning review into platform behavior.

## What to review

Reviewers should evaluate:

- whether the standard clearly rejects artist replacement
- whether AI-use disclosure expectations are specific enough
- whether collaborator consent boundaries are strong enough
- whether credit expectations are clear
- whether the rejected patterns are complete
- whether the review rubric avoids certification, verification, or endorsement claims
- whether future platform capabilities are properly gated by governance

## What not to review here

This packet is not for:

- requesting accounts
- submitting projects
- joining projects
- contacting collaborators
- asking for certification
- requesting marketplace features
- uploading AI assets

## Review format

A useful review should include:

1. Area reviewed
2. What works
3. What is unclear
4. Risk or abuse case
5. Suggested change
6. Why the change preserves the standard
