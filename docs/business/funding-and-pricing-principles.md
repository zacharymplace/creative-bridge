# Funding & Pricing Principles

Version: 2.9.0

The Creative Bridge can accept support only if funding does not corrupt the standard.

Payment must not buy:

- certification
- approval
- verification
- endorsement
- favorable review outcomes
- ranking
- public collaborator access
- governance power

## Phases

### Phase 0 — Public review era

Current phase.

No required payment.

Optional donations may support hosting, writing, maintenance, and review operations.

Not allowed:

- paid certification
- paid review outcomes
- paid project approval
- paid visibility

### Phase 1 — Supporter era

After meaningful external review.

Optional supporter memberships may sustain the public standard.

Not allowed:

- governance control
- review priority
- certification
- endorsement
- creator ranking

### Phase 2 — Toolkit era

After the standard proves useful.

Paid templates, implementation packs, workshops, and advisory materials can exist around the free standard.

Not allowed:

- locking the standard itself
- selling approval
- pay-to-legitimize workflows

### Phase 3 — Institutional era

Only after governance hardening.

Organizations may fund implementation, training, or stewardship without controlling the standard.

Not allowed:

- sponsor influence over rejected patterns
- sponsor influence over governance decisions
- sponsor influence over review outcomes
- sponsor influence over public claims

## Pricing ladders

- Donation: $5–$25 one-time or monthly
- Supporter: $5–$15/month
- Builder Toolkit: $29–$99 one-time
- Workshop: $250–$1,500/session
- Institutional Support: Custom

## Principle

Funding should sustain the work without becoming a legitimacy market.
