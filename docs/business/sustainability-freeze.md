# Sustainability Freeze

Version: 3.0.0

The Sustainability Freeze marks the boundary between public-standard development and future sustainability experiments.

The Creative Bridge can consider funding only after the rules for funding have been made public.

## Frozen layers

### Public standard

The core public standard, trust layer, rejected patterns, review rubric, governance layer, system map, and limits statement remain free to read.

### Review operating system

Public review, review intake, reviewer briefs, launch copy, feedback seeds, response logs, abuse cases, incentive drift, and stress tests are established before monetization.

### Rationale layer

Whyfiles explain the reasoning behind rules, rejections, guardrails, and platform constraints.

### Funding principles

Donation, supporter, toolkit, workshop, and institutional paths are defined with guardrails against certification, approval, ranking, review priority, and governance capture.

## Payment boundary

There are currently:

- no payment links
- no donation buttons
- no subscriptions
- no paid review priority
- no paid certification
- no paid endorsement
- no paid visibility
- no sponsor influence over rejected patterns or governance decisions

## Future requirements

Any future payment experiment must:

- cite the Funding & Pricing Principles
- preserve free access to the core standard
- avoid governance influence or review priority
- keep paid toolkits separate from certification or endorsement
- include non-interference boundaries for institutional support
- be logged as a governance-relevant decision

## Next phase

Future v3.x work may introduce donation readiness, supporter copy, implementation toolkit outlines, or sponsorship guardrails.

Actual payment mechanics should remain gated until the public review loop receives real external feedback.
