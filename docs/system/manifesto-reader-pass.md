# Manifesto Reader Pass

Version: 3.41.0

The Manifesto page should explain the project’s operating philosophy without drifting into hype, authority, or moral superiority.

## Decision

Refine `/manifesto` into a practical principles-and-posture guide.

## Reader order

- What the manifesto is for
- Core beliefs
- What the manifesto does not claim
- Operating posture
- Escalation paths

## Principle

A manifesto should clarify posture and values without becoming authority language or launch theater.
