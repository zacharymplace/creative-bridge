# Donation Provider Decision Reader Pass

Version: 3.45.0

The Donation Provider Decision page should explain the provider decision record without implying donation launch, funding activation, or permanent approval.

## Decision

Refine `/donation-provider-decision` into a practical provider decision and traceability guide.

## Reader order

- What the decision record is for
- What the decision does not mean
- Decision factors
- Required safeguards
- Escalation paths

## Principle

A provider decision should document why a path was selected without turning selection into launch permission.
