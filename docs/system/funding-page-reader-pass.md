# Funding Page Reader Pass

Version: 3.21.0

The Funding page is the highest-risk interpretation zone.

## Decision

Refine `/funding` around reader comprehension before any payment mechanics are added.

## Reader order

- What funding is for
- What funding cannot buy
- Allowed support models
- Prohibited support interpretations
- Current implementation status
- Where to go next

## Principle

Funding should support maintenance, not purchase legitimacy, priority, approval, or influence.
