# Donation Launch Review Reader Pass

Version: 3.48.0

The Donation Launch Review page should evaluate launch readiness without implying that review alone authorizes activation.

## Decision

Refine `/donation-launch-review` into a practical final-review and launch-risk guide.

## Reader order

- What launch review is for
- What launch review does not mean
- Review dimensions
- Launch blockers
- Escalation paths

## Principle

A launch review should surface remaining risk before activation, not create momentum toward activation.
