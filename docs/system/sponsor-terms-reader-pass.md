# Sponsor Terms Reader Pass

Version: 3.52.0

The Sponsor Terms page should define what sponsorship can and cannot mean without creating endorsement, influence, priority, or governance power.

## Decision

Refine `/sponsor-terms` into a practical sponsor-boundaries and non-interference guide.

## Reader order

- What sponsorship is for
- What sponsorship cannot buy
- Required sponsor boundaries
- Unsafe sponsor signals
- Escalation paths

## Principle

Sponsorship can support operations, but it cannot purchase authority, review outcomes, governance influence, or public trust status.
