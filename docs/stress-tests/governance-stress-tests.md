# Governance Stress Tests

Creative Bridge should be evaluated by whether it can resist incentive drift after success, not only whether it works while small.

## Core Question

Can this standard survive success?

## Stress Test Categories

### Success Pressure

A high-profile creator joins and pulls attention toward status rather than stewardship.

Guardrail:
Review must remain project-centered, not creator-centered.

### Engagement Drift

Activity volume, reactions, and visibility start becoming quality proxies.

Guardrail:
Reject engagement as a primary quality signal.

### Marketplace Capture

Collaboration turns into transactional matching.

Guardrail:
Keep role requests bounded, consent-based, and non-extractive.

### AI Slop Flood

Low-effort AI-generated submissions overwhelm review.

Guardrail:
Require authorship disclosure, project specificity, and evidence of situated intent.

### Governance Deadlock

Reviewers disagree without a resolution path.

Guardrail:
Use escalation tiers, written dissent, and reversible decisions.

### Monetization Pressure

Revenue incentives push toward rankings, premium access, or visibility boosts.

Guardrail:
Separate sustainability funding from quality recognition.
